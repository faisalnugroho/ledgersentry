About disputes

The Open dispute button above writes a dispute deadline on-chain (now + window, node clock). Resolution is impossible until the deadline passes; dispute evidence stays acceptable until resolution. Blank disputes are allowed; evidence you add must carry its own commitment.

KNOW WHAT THE RECORD MEANS

Deliberately bounded. Openly inspectable.

A digest proves the bytes were not altered — it cannot prove the artifact is honest. Anyone may append evidence; this is not Sybil-resistant. The model interprets requirements and can be wrong; INCONCLUSIVE is a first-class outcome, not an error.

Resolution is permissionless but never rushes a dispute: the deadline is enforced by the contract from the node-assigned clock. Terminal records are immutable; escalation means a fresh audit. This prototype never moves funds.
